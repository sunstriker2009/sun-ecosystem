# SUN branding policy v2.0

**Status:** Approved  
**Date:** 2026-11-18

## Decision

Adopt purpose-led visual themes for governance and orchestration, productivity tools, and application products. Use a recognizable SUN arc, the “by Sun” endorsement and consistent visual proportions to establish the common identity. Keep product silhouettes and palettes distinct. The [Brand Guidelines](../branding/BRAND-GUIDELINES.md) define the family themes and the [Product Repository Branding Standard](../docs/governance/PRODUCT-BRANDING.md) governs implementation.

## CodeFolio

CodeFolio by Sun is the working example for the productivity theme alongside WORKBENCH. The selected artwork uses an open folio with code brackets in the pages and a tapered golden sunrise arc above the spine. The smallest icon exports give the arc more weight for legibility. The palette is aubergine, warm ivory and apricot. The artwork uses “Find it. Make it yours.”

## Adoption

Review existing product lines against the published direction and track any updates in their own branding PRs. Registry, naming and repository provisioning changes follow their existing processes.
