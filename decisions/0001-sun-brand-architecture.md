# Decision 0001 — SUN Brand Architecture

**Date:** 2026-10-30  
**Status:** Accepted

## Context
The product portfolio needs a coherent identity without coupling independent applications and developer libraries.

## Decision
Use **SUN** as the umbrella brand; endorse public products as **PRODUCT by Sun** and use **Sun.<Capability>** for technical namespaces. Public product names may differ from technical identifiers. A product name requires explicit approval; proposals do not authorize implementation. Keep branding governance here and technical implementation in individual repositories.

## Consequences
A shared visual language and registry improve consistency; product independence is preserved. Commercial clearance is required separately before public release.
