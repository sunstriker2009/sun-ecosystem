# SUN Visual Identity — v1.0 Direction

**Status:** Shared visual direction accepted; specific logos, fonts, and colors are provisional.

## Design principles
Minimal, geometric, contemporary. Deep dark surfaces with restrained luminous accents; subtle celestial or optical symbolism. Consistent typography, spacing, and icon proportions. Avoid detailed illustrations or making every product look like a planet.

Proposed product accents: ORYVA gold (#F6C453), ORBIT electric blue (#4F8CFF), NOVA violet (#A78BFA), PRISM spectral multicolor, PULSAR cyan (#22D3EE), LUMEN emerald (#34D399), BEACON orange (#FB923C), FORGE red (#F87171). **Proposed product names and accent choices are not approvals.**

## Logo principles
- Work at 24×24 pixels.
- Provide monochrome and full-color variants.
- Avoid relying on gradients for recognition.
- Use common geometric construction and consistent optical weight.
- Products must work without the SUN endorsement.
- Typography candidate: Inter or a similarly readable modern sans serif (not yet finalized).

## ORYVA exploration
Consider an abstract protective ring, luminous boundary, or trust symbol. Do not reuse third-party IAM logos or assume the former HALO brand's mark is available.
