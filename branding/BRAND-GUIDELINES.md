# SUN Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Approved:** 2026-10-30

## Vision
SUN is the parent brand for a family of independently usable software products, libraries, frameworks, and developer tools. Products share a recognizable identity without requiring technical dependencies on one another.

**Brand concept:** One source. Many independent products.

**Working parent-brand tagline:** One Source. Infinite Possibilities. (Not yet independently approved as a final marketing tagline.)

## Brand architecture
- Parent: **SUN** (visual/umbrella identity).
- Endorsement: **PRODUCT by Sun** (e.g., ORYVA by Sun).
- Public product identities remain independent.
- Technical namespaces use **Sun.<Capability>**, not necessarily the public product name.
- External applications may say **Powered by PRODUCT** after integration, subject to product branding guidance.

## Brand character
Modern, sophisticated, dependable, minimal, geometric, and internationally approachable. Inspiration includes astronomy, planetary systems, light, optics, energy, motion, and exploration; literal celestial terminology is not mandatory.

## Portfolio policy
A product name is appropriate for an independently useful platform, application, or named developer product. Small shared utilities may retain ordinary technical names without a distinct public brand.

MarketPilot AI remains an independent application and may be described as **Powered by ORYVA** after integration. It is not automatically part of the SUN portfolio.

## Governance
- Approved names and proposed names must always be distinguished.
- Commercial clearance is separate from internal naming approval.
- Product architecture, coding, and release tracking belong in product-specific repositories.
- Record substantive brand changes in decisions/ and CHANGELOG.md.
