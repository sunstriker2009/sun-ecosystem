# SUN Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 2.0  
**Approved:** 2026-11-18

## Vision
SUN is the parent brand for a family of independently usable software products, libraries, frameworks, and developer tools. Products share a recognizable identity without requiring technical dependencies on one another.

**Brand concept:** One source. Many independent products.

**Working parent-brand tagline:** One Source. Infinite Possibilities. (Not yet independently approved as a final marketing tagline.)

## Brand architecture
- Parent: **SUN** (visual/umbrella identity).
- Endorsement: **ProductName by Sun** (for example, **Outpost by Sun**).
- Public product identities remain independent. Use the [approved display casing](NAMING-GUIDELINES.md#display-names-and-artwork) in prose, interfaces, registries, and galleries.
- Technical namespaces use **Sun.<Capability>**, not necessarily the public product name.
- External applications may say **Powered by ProductName** after integration, subject to product branding guidance.

## Brand character
SUN is modern, dependable, and understated. Use clear typography, restrained geometric forms, and approachable language. Draw names and visual ideas from what a product does, the work it supports, or a metaphor that helps explain its purpose. A visual reference to the Sun and the “by Sun” endorsement connect the family across distinct visual themes.

## Product families and visual themes

Build each central symbol around the product’s purpose and give its identity a recognizable visual connection to the Sun that fits the composition. CodeFolio uses a sunrise arc above the folio, and Workbench uses sunlight as part of its artwork. A sunrise, a sunlit edge, or another recognizable expression of the Sun can serve the same role. Use the **“by Sun”** endorsement in endorsed lockups and keep typography, spacing, and optical proportions consistent across the family. Distinguish individual products through their silhouettes, palettes, and imagery.

| Family | Registry series | Examples | Visual direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Governance and orchestration | `SUN-GNNN` | Strata, Foundry, Foundry Pilot | Structural forms, layered materials and grounded compositions suggest foundations, tooling and coordination. |
| Productivity tools | `SUN-PNNN` | Workbench, CodeFolio | Draw from the work people do, such as opening a reference, editing a parameter, or organizing and running scripts. |
| Application components | `SUN-CNNN` | Outpost | Develop each theme from the application’s purpose. Outpost’s orbital outpost imagery expresses a protected point of access for an identity and security platform. |

Use these families to guide visual design. Consult the [Product Repository Branding Standard](../docs/governance/PRODUCT-BRANDING.md) for concept review, asset delivery and adoption.

## Portfolio policy
A product name is appropriate for an independently useful platform, application, or named developer product. Small shared utilities may retain ordinary technical names without a distinct public brand.

MarketPilot AI remains an independent application and may be described as **Powered by Outpost** after integration. It is not automatically part of the SUN portfolio.

## Governance
- Approved names and proposed names must always be distinguished.
- Commercial clearance is separate from internal naming approval.
- Product architecture, coding, and release tracking belong in product-specific repositories.
- Record substantive brand changes in decisions/ and CHANGELOG.md.
- Follow the [SUN Git Workflow & Merge Policy v1.0](../docs/governance/GIT-WORKFLOW.md) for changes across ecosystem and product repositories; keep repository-specific implementations outside this branding repository.
