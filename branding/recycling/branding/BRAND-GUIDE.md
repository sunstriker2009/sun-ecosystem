# SUN Parent Brand Guide — v1

**Brand:** SUN — The Product Ecosystem

**Tagline:** One source. Many independent products.

**Role:** Parent identity for the SUN product family, not a standalone product.

## Owner-approved original artwork

- `branding/assets/readme-banner.png`: selected second panoramic concept, 2172 × 724 pixels.
- `branding/assets/symbol.png`: selected third symbol concept, 1254 × 1254 pixels.
- `branding/archive/originals/`: preserve exact copies of both originals.

These PNG files are authoritative. Compact SVG adaptations must be labeled as simplified, not exact extractions. The active filenames are stable and unversioned. Byte-identical, versioned originals are retained at `branding/archive/originals/readme-banner-v1.png` and `branding/archive/originals/symbol-canonical-v1.png`. New revisions must preserve prior originals in the archive before replacing active assets.

## Palette

Midnight navy `#06163D`, solar gold `#FFCA39`, warm white `#F5F7FC`, amber `#F29B18`.

## README

The approved panoramic banner appears at the top of README.md. The earlier exploratory image is no longer displayed there; its concept notes remain available as historical exploration.

## Governance

No product registry rename. No new product ID. Preserve existing governance, naming decisions and historical brand concepts.
## Active asset directory convention

Only `branding/assets/symbol.png` and `branding/assets/readme-banner.png` are active parent-brand images at the root of `branding/assets/`. Exploratory images, legacy SVG adaptations, and their reference READMEs are kept under `branding/assets/refs/` (including `refs/vector/`). These are reference materials, not production assets. Byte-identical versioned original PNGs remain preserved separately under `branding/archive/originals/`.
