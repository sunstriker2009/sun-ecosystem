# SUN Ecosystem Branding Policy v2 Draft

**Status:** Working draft for discussion  
**Active references:** [SUN Brand Guidelines v1.0](../../branding/BRAND-GUIDELINES.md) and [Product Repository Branding Standard v1.0](PRODUCT-BRANDING.md)

In SUN branding discussions, **draft policy** refers to this document while v2 is being shaped. The v1.0 documents govern current branding until v2 is approved and published.

## Shared SUN identity

Proposed family cues are the recognizable SUN arc, the “by Sun” endorsement and consistent typography and proportions. Develop the central symbol around the product’s purpose. Give each family its own visual theme, and use product-specific silhouettes, palettes and imagery to distinguish products within a family.

## Product families

| Family | Current examples | Proposed visual direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Governance and orchestration | STRATA, FOUNDRY, Foundry Pilot | Use structural forms, layered materials and grounded compositions to communicate foundations, tooling and coordination. |
| Productivity tools | CodeFolio, WORKBENCH | Draw marks and imagery from the work people do. Use cues such as opening a reference, editing a parameter or arranging and running scripts in a workspace. |
| Application products | OUTPOST | Develop a theme around each application’s purpose. OUTPOST’s orbital outpost imagery can express a protected point of access for an identity and security platform. |

These examples establish the design direction for discussion. The exact palettes, artwork and any additional families can be refined before publication.

## CodeFolio direction

**CodeFolio** is the chosen product name and **CodeFolio by Sun** is its endorsed brand lockup. It is a searchable, interactive snippet reference inspired by SnippetBook, with rich examples and a builder that lets users edit parameters, copy the generated snippet or save named variants for reuse.

- **Symbol concept** — Two open pages shaped like angle brackets, with a SUN arc between them. Use a simplified page-and-arc mark for small placements.
- **Palette concept** — Aubergine, warm ivory and apricot.
- **Working tagline** — “Find it. Make it yours.”
- **Banner concept** — Show an example snippet with an editable parameter highlighted to make the builder visible.

**Current product names:** WORKBENCH succeeds ASTELVO. OUTPOST succeeds ORYVA. Use the current names in new branding work.

## Publication and adoption

1. Refine the family themes and shared visual cues in this draft.
2. Approve and publish v2 as a coordinated revision of the current ecosystem branding guidelines and repository branding standard.
3. Review existing product lines against the approved revision. Record any needed updates to product brand guides, registry and gallery references, or approved visual assets. Apply those updates through the normal repository PR and artwork approval processes.
